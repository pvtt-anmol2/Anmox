Who I Am

I'm Anmol — a student who fell in love with the idea that a few lines of code can turn into something people actually use. What started as curiosity about how websites work has grown into a genuine obsession with programming, hackathons, and everything artificial intelligence.

Right now, I'm deep in the process of learning full stack development — piecing together frontend craft with backend logic, one project at a time. I'm not interested in shortcuts. I'd rather understand why something works than just copy what works.

Somewhere down the line, I want to build my own startup — something that starts as a weekend idea and turns into a product people rely on. Until then, I'm building, breaking, fixing, and building again.

I believe consistency beats talent.
